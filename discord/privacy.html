Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

SR Link links a Discord account to a Sub Rosa account and lets Discord servers give a role to linked players. It is run by noche (noche-x on GitHub) at https://srdiscord.link.

Collected data

When you generate a link code with /link or app commands:

  • Your Discord user ID and display name.
  • A 6 digit code tied to them. It expires after 5 minutes.

When you enter the code on the link server in Sub Rosa:

  • Your Steam ID and Sub Rosa account ID, as given by the game.
  • Your ingame name and ingame phone number.
  • The time the link was made.

When you use Linked Roles:

  • A Discord access token and refresh token with the identify and role_connections.write scopes. I use them only to show your Discord username after sign-in and to set the Sub Rosa account linked value on your profile.

When you visit this site or use the install flow, the web server and reverse proxy record standard request data: IP address, user agent, time and requested URL. The site sits behind Cloudflare, which also processes your requests.

I do not read your messages, servers, friends, email address or any other Discord content as seen by the permissions on the Discord app.

How it is used

  • To link your Discord and Sub Rosa accounts and show the link's status.
  • To update your Linked Roles value so servers can give a role.
  • To stop abuse, such as repeated wrong codes, and to keep the service working.

I do not sell your data or use it for advertising. I share it only with the services needed to run the app: Discord, Cloudflare and my hosting provider.

How long it is kept

  • Link codes: deleted when they expire or when you generate a new one.
  • Links (Discord ID and name, Steam ID, ingame name, phone number): kept until you ask me to delete them. Links are meant to be permanent and one to one.
  • OAuth tokens: kept while Linked Roles is connected, and deleted when Discord rejects them or you ask me to.
  • Server logs: kept for a limited time for security and debugging, then deleted.

Your choices

You can remove SR Link at any time in Discord under Settings, Authorized Apps, which revokes its access. To delete the stored link, tokens and any other data I hold about you, contact me with your Discord ID and I will remove it.

Security

Data is stored on a server I control and is not exposed publicly. No system is perfectly secure, and I can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required to use Discord or Sub Rosa.

Changes

I may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
